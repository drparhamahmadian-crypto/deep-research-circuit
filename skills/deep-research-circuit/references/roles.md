# Roles for substantial investigations

Use one coordinator and no recursive teams. Delegate independent parts only when useful and authorised, with no more than three workers at once. Use one dispatch owner: check pending, running and resumable workers before dispatch; reserve capacity for the next research and review stage. Explicitly supersede obsolete assignments and verify retirement before reusing capacity. Give reviewers the raw evidence and access needed to challenge the enquiry, early findings and final claims. Avoid duplicate retrieval unless it resolves a consequential uncertainty. Keep capacity for a reviewer; researchers and the fresh writer need not run simultaneously.

Combine coordination, research and writing where suitable. Add separate production roles only for a specific need or user request; preserve the independent challenge, claim review and clearance below.

Normally one independent reviewer can challenge the plan, inspect the first consequential evidence and review the final draft. Obtain these checks at the existing stage returns, not through continuous polling. Additional independent rounds require a user request or a concrete unresolved concern that a second assessment can address; a development test's two rounds are not a default for every enquiry. Give reviewers the request, applicable checks and raw evidence without the producer's reasoning history or preferred verdict.

**Coordinator:** retain the original request and constraints. Assign each worker a specific answerable question, permitted work, relevant method/skill, evidence requirements and an ending checkpoint. Supply the actual request and relevant raw evidence, not just the coordinator's preferred interpretation. Verify that the worker can access its sources. Use short stages that end before another dependent stage is assigned; do not assume continuous visibility into a running worker.

At each checkpoint, check both the evidence found and the material scope still missing. Give concrete feedback: what is out of scope, which premise changed, and the next question that could change the answer. Do not delegate all judgement to final review. Compare cost, coverage and unknowns without treating reviewer agreement or extra searches as measured improvement.

The coordinator owns extension decisions. A worker names the unanswered question, specific next action, expected decision value, retained leads and requested units. Grant the smallest useful next stage or explain refusal using evidence; “allowance used” is not a refusal reason. Explicit user ceilings remain binding. Review the result before another stage. Under active hooks, only the host-identified coordinator can grant, dispose of leads and propose closure through [search-extensions.md](search-extensions.md). Closure still requires independent review.

**Researcher:** use the assigned subject method. Return a compact handoff: question answered; source and passage/table/location; finding versus inference; decision-changing uncertainty; next necessary check. For load-bearing citations include the evidence receipt in [document-recovery.md](document-recovery.md), including URL origin and recovery limits. At evidence handoffs, flag failed or partial reads of load-bearing sources and applicable checks omitted; the coordinator checks consequential omissions before the next dependent stage. End at the assigned checkpoint rather than launching additional stages.

**Independent reviewer:** challenge the initial enquiry and first consequential evidence, then assess the final load-bearing claims. Check scope, source support, contradictions, applicable subject methods and whether the next search would change the decision. Inspect citation receipts against the recovered content; a source's existence or tool success alone does not clear a claim. If the passage or receipt is unavailable, withhold clearance of the affected claim and state what is missing. Report any concrete errors or decision-changing unresolved questions; if none are found, say so within the scope checked. Do not demand agreement with a preferred conclusion. Separate supported objections from hypotheses. Check for a consequential omission using [source-coverage.md](source-coverage.md) when needed. State the claims and coverage actually checked, any borrowed source evidence, and unresolved limits; clearance is scoped, not a guarantee of completeness. Independence means a separate agent and assessment, not statistically independent errors.

**Writer:** synthesize the accepted evidence without inventing facts or hiding unresolved objections. Give the claim reviewer the draft and source locations; compressed summaries alone are insufficient evidence. Use a separate fresh writer when requested or when independent synthesis serves a specific purpose. Then use an actually fresh context, without inherited conversation history, and supply the original request and accepted evidence. If required separation is unavailable, disclose it; do not simulate it by changing persona.

During claim review, compare each decisive number with its original value, unit, population/entity and period; a nearby correct quote does not clear other numbers in the sentence. Check each required entitlement or condition before calling a candidate eligible or best-fitting. Distinguish unresolved user choices from accepted constraints. Retain the strongest unverified source/alternative and why it could matter.

For guarded runs, inspect the current `research-scope.json` alongside raw source returns. Challenge premature closure, unsupported lead dismissals, unnecessary extensions and refusals that merely repeat the allowance. Check whether an unregistered source or unanswered part of the request could change the recommendation. Approve the scope claim only when the report explains what the checked evidence permits and preserves remaining material uncertainty. Review it in the same bound attestation as the substantive claims; a second review ceremony is unnecessary.

**Claim/source gate:** preserve these roles. The researcher/coordinator prepares saved evidence and the manifest; the writer marks load-bearing claims in the exact draft. The independent reviewer inspects the full draft, source passages and scope, then completes the hash-bound review template in [claim-validation.md](claim-validation.md). A different identifier alone does not prove independence. The coordinator runs `check` and uses `render` for the delivered text only after clearance. Corrected evidence, manifest or draft requires a fresh bound assessment. Reviewers must check consequential unmarked prose too; the script cannot identify every factual assertion or validate semantic entailment. Do not describe a local checker as a global interception or Stop hook.

Batch review findings into decision-changing corrections and optional improvements. Resolve the material corrections together before renewed clearance; stylistic preferences alone do not trigger more research. Reassessment may start with the changes and affected conclusions, reusing unchanged evidence and prior findings. The independent reviewer still checks the final full draft for coverage and propagated effects and personally issues a new attestation for its exact files. Never copy an old approval onto new hashes. Stop a correction cycle when the objection is resolved or the conclusion is appropriately narrowed; an inaccessible fact need not be chased indefinitely.

**Intervention:** the coordinator inspects stage returns and intervenes promptly when evidence changes scope or a premise. Hold affected work across all roles. If available, interrupt workers doing invalidated work; quarantine late output until assessed. Before a correction, preserve the affected evidence/draft files and hashes. If an original is missing, label the gap rather than reconstructing it. Assign only the bounded investigation needed to resolve an objection. Acknowledgement is not clearance: a qualified independent reviewer must assess the correction or evidence-based rejection before affected dependent work resumes. The coordinator cannot self-clear. Keep unresolved disagreement visible and withhold the affected conclusion; do not force agreement or search indefinitely. Other supported findings can still be delivered.

Replace an unsuitable or inaccessible reviewer with another qualified agent. Do not simulate independent review by changing the coordinator's persona. If no qualified replacement is available, disclose this and withhold the affected decision-driving conclusion; still deliver useful supported findings. Report runtime controls and review actually performed, without claiming technical enforcement from these instructions.
