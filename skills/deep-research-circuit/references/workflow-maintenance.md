# Revising this workflow

Use only for an authorised revision after a material capability change or repeated failure, not ordinary research or every model release.

Resolve the current skill-creator if available. Inspect the actual change or failure before adding instructions. Preserve evidence standards, permission boundaries and required independent checks; a stronger model alone does not justify removing them.

Keep the evidence contract, permissions and independent assessment stable across model releases. Resolve current model/tool capabilities when needed; avoid model-name pins, a mandatory provider fallback or a redesign merely because a newer model appears. Allow better models to choose better questions and methods within the same evidence and stopping requirements.

Use existing failure records, reviews and regression evidence first. A wording or orchestration correction does not automatically require another live research exercise. Verify only what changed: instruction/packaging checks for text changes, affected behavioural regressions for executable changes, and a bounded new exercise only for an important behaviour the existing evidence cannot establish. Rebinding unchanged files is not a new efficacy test.

Before claiming that a workaround can safely be retired or that quality/speed improved, compare relevant routine and difficult cases using comparable inputs and stated criteria. Assess factual errors, unsupported claims, coverage and resource use. Record limits and unresolved objections; packaging checks, verbosity and reviewer agreement do not prove improvement. A plausible efficiency change can be released as unmeasured without inventing a saving or repeatedly commissioning benchmarks.

Keep only guidance whose benefit justifies its cost. No background monitoring, self-update, uninstall or permission expansion is authorised by this procedure.
