# Historical accounts

Identify origin, author/vantage, event date versus recording date and edition or translation. Distinguish contemporary observation, later recollection and subsequent interpretation. Inspect context around the cited passage.

Trace dependence: copied accounts, quotations from a shared source and summaries of the same archive are not independent corroboration. Consider contrary accounts and missing records; explain why a source supports this particular claim rather than treating primary status as automatic truth. Label access gaps and disputed interpretations. Match any specialist historical skill to the period and source type before loading it.
