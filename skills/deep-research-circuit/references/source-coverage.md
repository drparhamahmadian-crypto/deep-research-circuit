# Source coverage

When missing evidence could change the answer, or before an absence or completeness claim, identify the expected authoritative sources and the scope the enquiry can establish. Record sources actually reached, failed or partial reads, and material search or date limits. An empty search is a result within that scope, not proof that nothing exists elsewhere.

Name the most consequential possible omission and choose one distinct route that could reveal it: for example an operational authority rather than a supplier, a local-language record, or a counterexample. Use the route that fits the omission; record what it establishes and what remains unchecked. Do not add queries merely to increase source count. If the gap cannot change the decision, report the limitation. Keep “not found within the checked scope” distinct from “does not exist”. Do not require exhaustive searching or a full coverage audit for a direct lookup.

Reserve the final quarter of each approved discovery/retrieval stage for inspecting its strongest unresolved leads. Use the original stage first; an extension requires the coordinator's reasoned decision and cannot exceed a user hard ceiling. Preserve a promising source when first found: locator, why it may matter, inspected versus uninspected status, and the next missing check. A failed extraction, low search rank, unfamiliar terminology or initially incomplete match is not by itself grounds to discard potentially decisive evidence. Exclude it only for an evidenced scope/quality reason. Carry material unresolved leads into handoffs and the final answer; do not let compression or a neat shortlist erase them.

With the active guard, register material leads using [search-extensions.md](search-extensions.md). The guard retains original details and decision history. Budget exhaustion alone cannot dispose of a lead or close the enquiry. The independent reviewer checks dismissed leads and extension refusals against the evidence, and compares the ledger with source returns for consequential omissions. The guard cannot discover a source the researcher failed to register. Without hooks, use existing working notes and disclose that this retention is procedural.
