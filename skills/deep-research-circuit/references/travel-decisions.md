# Actionable travel comparisons

Use for practical itineraries or priced offers; destination curiosity can stay exploratory. Preserve local departure/arrival and check-in/out dates, airports, travellers, accessibility needs, baggage and flexibility. Compare whole-party cost with required extras, transfers and necessary overnight stays. Mark unknown amounts instead of calling a partial price all-in.

For flexible flights, begin with date grids or multi-date tools from complementary flight-comparison services where accessible, then verify selected offers with sellers. Record the date/duration regions, travellers, currency and filters actually screened, plus material unavailable prices. One service, repeated syndication or a cache timestamp does not establish independent coverage or a fresh seller quote. Do not describe any aggregator as covering every airline or fare. Check its commercial role when independence matters. An airline is useful for verification but does not establish the best market deal.

Sample promising regions, compare credible cost/convenience alternatives and revisit gaps that could change the choice. Distinguish a lower base fare from a cheaper qualifying trip. Use the user's priorities without inventing willingness-to-pay thresholds. Check a relevant membership or professional discount only when eligibility and possible net saving warrant it; do not assume entitlement or recommend joining from an advertised saving alone.

At the first capture, retain the visible query context alongside results: applied dates, airports, party, currency and relevant filters. For a modal or calendar that hides these fields, save the verified surrounding context with its association to that result; URL parameters alone are insufficient. Otherwise keep that result as a discovery lead with its missing scope. Screen hard constraints and decisive convenience/cost trade-offs before spending time on full seller terms. Deeply verify candidates that could change the recommendation, retaining material unverified rivals visibly. A conditional shortlist is a valid endpoint when further seller access is blocked; do not enumerate every date or provider to chase an unprovable global minimum.

Check the selected offer's live price, availability, baggage, payment and change/cancellation terms with its seller. Distinguish seller, marketing and operating carrier. Establish ticket and connection protection; a single purchase or multiple carriers alone does not determine it. Identify self-transfer, baggage recheck, airport changes and practical connection time. For accommodation, verify room/occupancy, mandatory charges and deposit/cancellation terms.

Before treating a flight as a qualifying priced choice, verify every requested direction (both for a return trip), whole-party price, required checked allowance per traveller on every sector, and ticket/connection terms. Do not infer these from an unexplained weight label or per-person advertisement alone. An anonymous quote may establish displayed terms; it does not establish held inventory or a final payment total. Separate-ticket itineraries may remain candidates, but disclose each ticket's baggage, transfer and disruption terms; do not automatically reject or describe them as protected.

Check local dates, time zones/daylight saving and elapsed times, including next-day home arrival. Verify all final requested dates after changing linked form fields and before submitting. Discard results from a wrong query. Distinguish flight-date span from nights at destination. If a source's price, schedule or allowance contradicts another field, resolve it with another suitable source or mark it unresolved; do not silently repair it and certify the offer.

Keep calendar/snippet leads, selected aggregator offers and seller quotations separate, with check times and unknown fees. Do not calculate a bag-inclusive total from a generic tariff unless applicability to the selected fare, sectors and travellers is established; otherwise label it an estimate. A blocked page is an access limitation, not evidence that the offer is absent. Preserve historical quotations with their original times; never relabel them as currently available.

For decisive offers, attach the price and terms to the source that displayed them and retain the exact selected-result evidence/location. Label a homepage or reconstructed search link as a navigation aid; it does not substantiate a fare. If the aggregator-to-seller handoff is not observed, record that limit rather than implying an observed handoff. Preserve the aggregator observation. Mark seller price, availability or terms verified only when inspected seller content supports that particular claim for the matching trip and check time. Follow [document-recovery.md](document-recovery.md) for the evidence receipt and bounded recovery; do not infer a dead offer, fabricated price or seller confirmation from the failed page alone.

Check destination/transit advice and traveller-specific entry rules with relevant official authorities. Record the check time and unresolved conditions; one aggregator does not establish the market's cheapest offer.

Match destination and season to the actual holiday: coast access, night-time comfort, rainfall and transfer burden may matter more than the gateway fare. Separate climate normals from forecasts. Price necessary onward travel before treating a city gateway as a complete beach option. Ask for traveller details only when needed for a decision or entry-rule check, not merely to access an optional quote.

Basis: [Google flight conditions](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/2475306?hl=en), [booking options](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/11583641?hl=en) and [Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/before-you-go). Accommodation checks are a design extension requiring provider terms. Accuracy gains are untested.
