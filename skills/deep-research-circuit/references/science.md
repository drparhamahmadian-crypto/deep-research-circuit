# Scientific questions

Select by the required operation, not the mere presence of a paper:

- **scientific-critical-thinking:** appraisal of a claim, study design, bias/confounding, statistical interpretation and applicability.
- **literature-review:** searching, screening and synthesising a body of evidence; link multiple reports of the same study. Formal systematic-review requirements apply when the task requires them.
- **paper-lookup:** scholarly discovery, identifiers and full-text retrieval; it does not replace appraisal.
- **biomedical-research-router:** additional biomedical source/method selection and species, exposure, assay or clinical translation checks when relevant.

Resolve these names against the current catalogue; do not assume installation or read all four. Separate retrieval from appraisal. If a required skill is unavailable, perform the applicable checks directly: design, population/exposure, outcome, denominator, effect and uncertainty, bias, overlapping data and applicability. Tag the evidence type. Distinguish association, mechanistic plausibility and demonstrated benefit; do not require a clinical framework for a non-clinical question. Name any check that actually could not be performed.
