# Business results and forecasts

Separate reported results, management guidance, analyst consensus and model projections. Reconcile period, currency, units, denominator and accounting basis against the original filing or statement. Preserve adjustments and comparability limitations.

For a prediction, expose material assumptions, incentives, uncertainty and decision-changing scenarios. Check historical calibration when relevant and available. Do not convert a forecast into an established outcome. A narrow claim check need not become a valuation exercise.

Use a financial-normalisation skill for actual normalisation work if available and proportionate. Reading its useful idea does not authorise invoking the whole workflow while ignoring its required dependencies. Distinguish published method guidance from evidence that an agent using it performs better.
