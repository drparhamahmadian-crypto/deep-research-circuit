# Carry status and uncertainty forward

Treat status as unknown until inspected evidence establishes it.

When a later conclusion, table, handoff or action depends on an unverified claim, preserve its uncertain status, supporting source and missing verification. Do not silently turn a tentative finding into a confirmed fact during compression. Update its status only when evidence resolves it.

For consequential legal dates, distinguish commencement, binding compliance deadline, consultation deadline, internal target and review date, as applicable. A verified rule alone does not prove a particular deadline binds. Confirm applicability, date and current status before calling a compliance obligation overdue; otherwise make the conclusion conditional.
